Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Quick answers for patients and visitors.

How can I book an appointment?

Choose a department, select a doctor, choose an available date and time, enter your details, and confirm the appointment.

Can I choose my doctor?

Yes. After selecting a department, you can choose an available doctor from that department.

How do I check my appointment?

Use your appointment reference number in the appointment lookup section to view the booking status.

Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes. Use your appointment reference in the lookup area and cancel it when the cancellation option is available.

What should I bring with me?

Bring your appointment reference and any relevant medical documents or previous reports.

How do I know my appointment is confirmed?

After successful booking, the website displays a confirmation page with your appointment reference number.
